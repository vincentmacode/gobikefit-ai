How it works

Three steps. No sensors, no appointments, no guesswork.

Step 1

Take two photos

Shoot a true side view on your bike — one with the pedal at 6 o'clock, one at 3 o'clock.

Step 2

On-device pose analysis

GoBikeFit measures your knee bend, torso lean, elbow angle, and knee-over-pedal position.

Step 3

Adjust with confidence

Get plain-English adjustment recommendations for your saddle, reach, and cleats.

What it measures

The adjustments that matter most for comfort, power, and injury prevention.

Saddle height

Dial in the knee bend that keeps you powerful and pain-free.

Reach & drop

Balance torso lean and elbow angle for a sustainable position.

Cleat position

Check knee-over-pedal-spindle (KOPS) alignment at 3 o'clock.

Re-fit anytime

Re-check your position after every adjustment until it's right.

Private by design

All analysis runs on your device. Your photos never leave your phone. No internet connection is required.
